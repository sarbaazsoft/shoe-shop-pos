### Feature: Dynamic Quick-Receive Payment Chips for Retail POS Terminal

Implement a smart, dynamic quick-receive amount chip component for the POS terminal payment screen that calculates and suggests cash-receiving denominations in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) specifically tailored for **retail stores** (exclude or disable for wholesale transactions).

#### 1. Applicability & Business Rule
- **Retail Only:** This quick-receive chip feature must **only** trigger for retail sales/store counters. If the transaction mode or customer profile is flagged as **Wholesale**, hide these chips entirely (as wholesale transactions typically involve bulk/exact invoice billing and different payment structures).

#### 2. Core Logic & Denominations (For Retail)
When a retail invoice amount is focused or updated, dynamically generate quick-tap chip buttons based on common Pakistani paper currency notes and retail rounding (Rs. 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 notes):

- **Strict Limit:** Display a **maximum of 4 chips** at any time to keep the UI clean and clutter-free.
- **For small retail invoices (e.g., up to Rs. 1,000):**
  - Suggest convenient round amounts (e.g., exact amount, next 100, next 500, or Rs. 1,000).
- **For medium retail invoices (e.g., Rs. 1,001 to Rs. 5,000):**
  - Suggest rounding up to the nearest Rs. 500 or Rs. 1,000 (e.g., if total is Rs. 3,250, suggest chips for `3,300`, `3,500`, `4,000`, and `5,000`).
- **For larger retail invoices (e.g., Rs. 5,001 to Rs. 10,000+):**
  - Suggest rounded notes like `10,000`, `15,000`, or convenient increments of Rs. 1,000 and Rs. 5,000.
- **Always Include:** An "Exact Amount" chip option as one of the choices alongside the rounded cash suggestions.

#### 3. UI & Interaction Requirements
- **Placement:** Render the maximum of 4 chips horizontally in a flex container directly above or beside the "Received Amount" input field in the POS payment modal.
- **Styling:** Design them as clean, clickable badges/pills with a subtle border and hover/active state matching the app's indigo/purple brand theme.
- **Action:** Tapping any chip should instantly populate the "Received Amount" input field with that value, automatically calculating the exact change due to the customer in real-time.
